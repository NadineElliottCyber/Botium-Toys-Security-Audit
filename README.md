# Botium Toys Security Audit

## Project Overview
This project documents an internal security audit conducted for the fictional company Botium Toys. The purpose of the audit was to assess the organization's existing security controls, identify potential risks, and evaluate compliance with industry standards and regulations.

## Audit Scope
The audit reviewed Botium Toys' security program, including:
- Employee devices and equipment
- Internal network and systems
- Data storage and retention
- Access controls
- Legacy systems
- Physical security controls

## Risk Assessment
The initial risk assessment identified a risk score of **8/10**, indicating a high level of risk due to gaps in security controls and compliance practices.

Key risks identified included:
- Lack of least privilege and separation of duties
- No intrusion detection system (IDS)
- No backups of critical data
- Customer credit card information was not encrypted
- Weak password requirements
- Inadequate asset management

## Controls Assessment
Existing and missing controls were evaluated across:
- Administrative/Managerial controls
- Technical controls
- Physical/Operational controls

## Compliance Review
The audit evaluated Botium Toys against:
- PCI DSS
- GDPR
- SOC 1 and SOC 2 best practices

## Recommendations
Based on the audit findings, recommended improvements include:
- Implement least privilege and separation of duties
- Encrypt sensitive customer and payment data
- Implement an intrusion detection system (IDS)
- Establish regular backups and disaster recovery procedures
- Strengthen password and access-control policies
- Improve asset inventory and classification
